Install
Paste it into Outlook.
Four steps, about two minutes. Do it once for each device where you write email.

  

    
01
Build
Choose your role, fill in your details, and check the preview in both Outlook themes.

    
02
Copy
Click Copy for Outlook at the top of the preview. There is one copy for both themes.

    
03
Paste
In Signatures, paste with Keep source formatting (Ctrl+V, then the paste options button). Plain Ctrl+V merges formatting and resets the text sizes.

    
04
Test
Send yourself a test, then switch Outlook between light and dark themes and check it on your phone.
